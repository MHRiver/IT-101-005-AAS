Bitaxe Mining Experiment

A small mining hardware experiment focused on setup and performance troubleshooting.

Bitaxe 601SHA-256Mining pool

System at a glance

Bitaxe→Network→Mining pool→Share reporting

What I built

  • Set up a Bitaxe 601 and investigated pool connectivity and reported hash rate.
  • Observed performance around 0.4 TH/s during troubleshooting.
  • Reported that restarting the device resolved the immediate performance issue.

Troubleshooting notes

Low reported hash rate

A restart improved the reported issue. A sustained benchmark and final tuning values have not been recorded.

What I learned

  • Short-term readings and long-term averages can tell different stories.
  • Record temperature, operating settings, and observation period when comparing performance.
