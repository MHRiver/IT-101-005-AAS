Cisco Switching Lab

Hands-on managed-switch configuration with a Cisco WS-C2960X-48LPS-L.

Cisco 2960XIOSVLANsSSH

System at a glance

Router / gateway→Cisco switch→VLAN / access ports→Lab devices

What I built

  • Connected to the switch through a USB serial console, including a Windows COM7 session.
  • Worked with VLAN 1, management addressing, access/trunk concepts, and links to another switch.
  • Configured the switch hostname riverswitch and explored SSH management.

Troubleshooting notes

Management access

Separated console access from IP-based management while learning initial switch configuration.

Uplink behavior

Investigated how access and trunk settings affect connectivity between switches. Final port assignments need to be recorded.

What I learned

  • A switch’s management IP and its Layer 2 forwarding serve different purposes.
  • Record port roles and VLAN membership alongside configuration changes.
