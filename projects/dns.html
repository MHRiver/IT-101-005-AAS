Pi-hole & Recursive DNS

Network-wide DNS filtering paired with a local recursive resolver.

Raspberry PiPi-holeUnboundDNS

System at a glance

LAN clients→Pi-hole→Unbound :5335→DNS hierarchy

What I built

  • Ran Pi-hole on a Raspberry Pi and configured Unbound as a local resolver at 127.0.0.1:5335.
  • Used query logs and direct DNS tests to investigate failing domains and upstream behavior.
  • Worked through IPv6 DNS bypass, DNSSEC settings, and differences between container and Raspberry Pi deployments.

Troubleshooting notes

SERVFAIL responses

Several domains failed while others resolved. Resolver settings and service restarts were part of the troubleshooting; the final complete configuration was not captured.

Clients bypassing Pi-hole

Investigated IPv6 DNS supplied by the router as a path around the configured Pi-hole resolver.

Filtering limits

DNS filtering cannot independently remove every advertisement served from the same domains as desired content.

What I learned

  • Test the client, Pi-hole, and upstream resolver independently.
  • A successful lookup does not prove every device uses the intended DNS server.
