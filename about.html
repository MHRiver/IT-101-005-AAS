THE PERSON BEHIND THE LAB

Hi, I'm Andy.

I'm an NJIT student exploring systems administration, networking, and infrastructure. I learn by building something, figuring out why it breaks, and working through the fix.

Why this notebook exists

A working project is only part of the story. These pages keep track of the decisions, connections, and troubleshooting that make the system understandable later.

What I work on

My projects span Python and SQL, Linux virtual machines, managed switching, DNS, remote access, reverse proxies, and self-hosted applications. My home lab gives me a place to connect those ideas.

What's next

Keep refining the existing projects and their documentation. Longer-term interests include multi-server applications with messaging, databases, and recovery workflows. Those ideas are future work, separate from the builds documented here.
